# Juniper Lane Bikes — Returns and refunds

Juniper Lane Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used to demonstrate this assistant.
None of these policies are real.

## Returning a bike

- You can return a bike within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- The bike must have been ridden for **no more than 50 km** and be undamaged.
  The odometer on our app-connected models records distance; for other models
  we inspect wear on the tyres and drivetrain.
- Custom builds and bikes fitted with customer-requested parts cannot be
  returned, but they are covered by the warranty.
- To start a return, email returns@juniperlane.example with your order number.
  We send a prepaid shipping label within one working day.

## Returning accessories

- Unused accessories (helmets, lights, locks, bags) can be returned within
  **60 days** in their original packaging.
- Helmets that have been worn cannot be returned, for hygiene and safety
  reasons.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 working days** of
  the return arriving at our workshop.
- Original delivery charges are refunded only if the item arrived faulty or
  was not what you ordered.
