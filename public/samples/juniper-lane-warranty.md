# Juniper Lane Bikes — Warranty and servicing

Juniper Lane Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used to demonstrate this assistant.

## Warranty

- **Frames:** lifetime warranty for the original owner against manufacturing
  defects.
- **Forks and other components:** 2 years.
- **Electric-bike batteries and motors:** 2 years or 500 charge cycles,
  whichever comes first.
- The warranty does not cover crash damage, normal wear (tyres, brake pads,
  chains, cassettes) or damage from fitting non-approved parts.
- To make a claim, send photos of the fault and your order number to
  warranty@juniperlane.example. We reply within 2 working days.

## Free first service

- Every new bike gets a **free first service within 3 months** of delivery.
  Cables stretch and bolts settle in the first few hundred kilometres.
- Book it at any partner workshop listed in your account, or post the bike to
  us with the prepaid label from your account page.

## Paid servicing

- **Basic service:** £45 — gears and brakes adjusted, safety check.
- **Full service:** £110 — everything in the basic service, plus drivetrain
  cleaned, bearings checked and wheels trued.
