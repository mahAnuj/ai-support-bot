# Juniper Lane Bikes — Delivery and shipping

Juniper Lane Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used to demonstrate this assistant.

## Delivery times

- **Standard delivery:** 3–5 working days. Free on orders over £150, otherwise £9.
- **Express delivery:** next working day if ordered before 2 pm. £19.
- Bikes are shipped **90% assembled**. You fit the pedals and straighten the
  handlebar; a 4 mm and a 15 mm wrench are included in the box.

## Where we deliver

- We deliver to the United Kingdom and Ireland only.
- We do **not** ship to other countries, including the rest of Europe.
- Orders to the Scottish Highlands and islands take 1–2 extra working days.

## Tracking and missed deliveries

- A tracking link is emailed when your order leaves the workshop.
- If you miss a delivery, the courier tries twice more on the following
  working days, then returns the parcel to us. We can resend it for £9.
